
# 📊 Executive Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Executive Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide a comprehensive overview of business sales performance. It enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, analyze revenue and profitability trends, evaluate product category performance, and identify top- and bottom-performing countries.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights, helping business executives make informed, data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance and profitability.
- Analyze monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming countries.
- Understand revenue contribution across different sales channels.
- Evaluate product category performance.
- Provide interactive filtering to explore sales data from different perspectives.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Analysis

### 1. Monthly Revenue and Profit Analysis
- Visualizes monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Helps identify changes in sales performance over time.
- Enables comparison between revenue generation and profitability.

### 2. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
- Identifies the five highest revenue-generating countries.
- Helps understand key geographical markets contributing to business revenue.

### 3. Bottom 5 Countries by Revenue
- Highlights the five lowest revenue-generating countries.
- Helps identify geographical markets that may require additional business attention.

### 4. Sales Channel Revenue Comparison
Analyzes revenue contribution across four sales channels:
- Online Store
- Corporate
- Retail Partners
- Marketplace

Displays the percentage contribution of each channel to total revenue.

### 5. Product Category Performance
Compares revenue contribution across five product categories:
- Hiking
- Camping
- Travel
- Cycling
- Fitness

Helps identify categories generating the highest revenue.

### 6. Geographical Sales Analysis
- Uses an interactive map to visualize geographical sales distribution.
- Helps understand the geographical presence of business revenue.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)

The dashboard includes the following interactive filters:

- **Quarter:** Analyze sales performance across different quarters.
- **Month Name:** Filter data by individual months.
- **Product Category:** Explore performance for specific product categories.
- **Country:** Analyze sales performance by geographical location.

These slicers allow users to dynamically explore the dashboard and perform focused analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – KPI calculations and business metrics.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **Data Modeling** – Establishing relationships between tables and structuring data for analysis.

## 💡 Business Value

This dashboard helps business stakeholders:

- Track revenue and profitability performance.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing markets.
- Understand customer demand across product categories.

- Make informed decisions using interactive data visualization.


## 🚀 How to Use

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using Power BI Desktop.
3. Interact with the dashboard using the available slicers.
4. Explore revenue, profitability, product category, sales channel, and geographical performance.
